        The Medical City Clinic is searching for a Nurse in Westgate Alabang to join the team and help lead the organization to the next level. This role is responsible for assisting the doctors in administering treatments, monitoring vital signs, handling medical emergencies, and providing critical assistance to patients as needed.


        This role reports directly to the Clinic Manager of TMC Clinic Westgate Alabang.


        What’s The Role All About?

        Job responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

        • Gathers and records patients' vital signs
        • Finds out and records patients' medical history
        • Ensures patients' safety and comfort
        • Develops appropriate nursing care plans
        • Performs triaging of patients
        • Performs routine procedures such as blood pressure measurements and injections
        • Documents data of patients through charting
        • Monitors inventory and ensures availability of related medical supplies
        • Submits census report every month


        Who Are We Looking For?

        An effective Staff Nurse must possess the following skills:

        • General knowledge in Nursing
        • Customer Service Skills
        • Proficient communication skills (verbal and written)


        Ideally, a candidate must be at least a graduate of Bachelor of Science in Nursing, must be a Registered Nurse (RN), preferably with at least 6 months clinical experience or on-the-job training in providing nursing care and support to patients.
